Groundwork of Understanding

Person A explains their experience, feelings, and desires. Person B reflects it back to check understanding. Once A's point is understood, B can begin to respond. Then A shares what was coming up for them, and B expresses their experience of those facets and what was coming up for them.

Phase 1 — Attachment & Regulation

  • Attend to feelings and needs
  • Being seen, being understood
  • Making amends, attending to needs
Goal: feel safe, seen, soothed, and understood.

Phase 2 — Collaborating & Mutual Care

  • How do we address these needs?
  • Negotiate solutions that feel like a win to both
  • Outcome may not be what either initially expected
Goal: a shared solution — help each other remember and maintain it.

Reminders

  • Reflect before responding
  • Understand first, then react
  • Commitments live in relationship, not in a vacuum
Tend the ground before planting.
